# Brand facts (sample knowledge base; public-style facts only, no internal data)

## Fresher AI program
FPT Software Academy runs a Fresher AI program for new graduates. Training covers AI fundamentals and GenAI, RAG engineering with LangChain, LLMOps and evaluation, and agentic AI with LangGraph. Freshers then join real projects for global clients.

## Company
FPT Software is the global IT services company of FPT Corporation, with over 30,000 employees serving more than 1,000 clients in over 30 countries. Japan is its largest market.

## AI strategy
FPT positions itself as an AI-first company. FleziPT is its AI-first platform for AI-augmented delivery. The FPT AI Factory, built with NVIDIA, provides GPU infrastructure in Vietnam and Japan.

## Culture
FPT's core values are Respect, Innovation, Teamwork, Fairness, Exemplary and Wisdom (Tôn trọng, Đổi mới, Đồng đội, Chí công, Gương mẫu, Sáng suốt).

## Go Japan
The Go Japan track prepares engineers to work with Japanese clients, including Japanese language training.

## Brand voice
Friendly, energetic and clear for Gen Z IT students. Always end with a call to action and the registration link. Hashtags always include #FPTSoftware.
